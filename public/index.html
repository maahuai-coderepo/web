Common Questions

Before You Lend Your Saree or Borrow a Lehenga

What if someone spills something or damages my outfit?
Every rental carries a refundable security deposit and timestamped pickup/return condition photos inside your booking thread. If a garment comes back damaged, Maahu's team reviews the photo evidence and resolves repair or compensation fairly before releasing the deposit.
What about lehenga blouse sizing and alterations?
Cutting or permanent alteration is never allowed. Only reversible, gentle hand-tacking stitches that open cleanly without leaving marks are permitted — and every piece lists clear measurements upfront.
Who takes care of dry cleaning?
Care expectations are shown clearly on every listing before you request or approve a booking, so lender and borrower are aligned from day one.
Will strangers see my home address or phone number?
Never publicly. Only Aadhaar QR verified women can book, and all messaging happens inside Maahu's private booking thread. Delivery and pickup details stay protected and scoped strictly to confirmed exchanges.
Can I share my closet only with close friends first?
Yes. Use your Digital Closet privately to track your wardrobe's value, or generate a private lookbook link to share with bridesmaids and friends on WhatsApp — even before opening pieces to the wider Mumbai circle.
