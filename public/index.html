The Problem

₹2–5 Lakh Sitting Folded. Doing Nothing.

The average urban Indian woman's wardrobe is worth between ₹2 and ₹5 lakh. Most of it is worn once — or never. Heritage sarees that should be circulating. Designer pieces gathering dust. Occasion wear bought in panic, abandoned after one function.

Maahu changes the equation. Your wardrobe becomes something worth sharing — pieces you no longer reach for finding a second life with someone who'll love them, in a circle you trust.

01
Audit Your Wardrobe
Know exactly what you own, what it's worth, and what your cost-per-wear is on every piece. Your closet has a number. Maahu helps you find it.
02
Lend What You Love
Your best pieces don't need to sit unworn between occasions. List them in the community and let them earn their place — in someone else's most important moments.
03
Borrow Without Buying
Access wardrobes you've been admiring. For a wedding, a work event, a trip — without adding another once-worn piece to your own cupboard.
Verified Members
Every Maahu member is verified. Community accountability — not anonymous strangers. The same model that drives India's 98% SHG repayment rate.
Agreed Terms
Every exchange has agreed terms upfront. Value, care, and what happens if something is damaged — all settled before anything changes hands.
Women-Only Community
Curated, invite-led. Every member shares the same relationship with Indian clothing — its value, its care, what it means to be trusted with something precious.
