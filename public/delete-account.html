Data Control & Store Safety

Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your Maahu account directly inside the mobile app or submit a deletion request on the web at any time.

Maahu AI Digital Private Limited · DPDP & App Store / Play Store Account Deletion Notice

Option 1: Delete Directly in the Maahu App (iOS & Android)

  • Open the Maahu app and sign in to your account.
  • Go to Profile → Help & Account → Privacy & Security / Delete Account.
  • Confirm the deletion request. Your account immediately enters deletion_pending status and all active marketplace access is revoked.

Option 2: Request Deletion via Web / Email

If you no longer have the app installed, email hello@maahu.ai from your registered email address or include your registered phone number with the subject line "Maahu Account Deletion Request". We verify ownership and process requests promptly.

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Deleted / Anonymized: Personal profile fields, Digital Closet items and garment photos, saved addresses, wishlist items, and notification tokens.
  • Temporarily Held Only If Applicable: Active rental obligations, open disputes/legal holds, and statutory payment/tax ledger records required under Indian law (purged automatically once statutory retention expires).
