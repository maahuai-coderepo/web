Community & Platform Rules

Terms of Service

Clear agreements protecting every lender, borrower, and Digital Closet user on Maahu.

Effective Date: November 2026 · Maahu AI Digital Private Limited · Mumbai, India

1. Eligibility & Verification

Maahu is a verified women-only community wardrobe and rental platform in India. Browse-only access may be available to registered accounts, while full marketplace lending, borrowing, closet publishing, and community posting require both an active account and verified identity via UIDAI Aadhaar Secure QR.

2. Digital Closet & AI Assistance

You retain full ownership of your garment photos and closet records. AI-suggested garment categories, descriptions, and valuation estimates are assistive only — you review and confirm all listing fields, rental rates, care instructions, and availability dates prior to publishing.

3. Bookings, Fees & Deposits

  • Upfront Pricing: Rental fee, refundable security deposit, applicable taxes, and Maahu's transparent platform fee are calculated server-side and displayed clearly before payment confirmation.
  • Handover Checkpoints: Both lender and borrower must capture condition photos at pickup and return via the booking workflow.
  • Refunds & Disputes: Undisputed security deposits refund automatically once clean return is confirmed. If damage, loss, or late return is flagged within the review window, Maahu's support and investigation team evaluates photo evidence and booking logs to determine fair settlement.

4. Community Conduct

Harassment, counterfeit misrepresentation, off-platform payment solicitation, unauthorized permanent alterations, or fraudulent identity submission may result in immediate account restriction or removal.

5. Contact

Questions about these Terms or a booking dispute can be directed to hello@maahu.ai.
