For Lenders

Put Your Wardrobe to Work

Your heritage pieces, your designer staples, your occasion wear — they shouldn't sit folded and idle between events. Maahu lets you lend what you love to women who will treat it the way you would.

01
Add Your Closet in Seconds
Snap photos of your pieces in your Digital Closet. Maahu tags category, fabric, and occasion automatically, shows your cost-per-wear, and lets you list any outfit in one tap.
02
Approve Verified Requests
Only Aadhaar QR verified women can request a booking. Review the borrower's community profile, dates, and upfront terms — you approve or decline every request.
03
Photo Handover & Protected Return
Both sides snap condition photos at pickup and return inside your booking chat. Once your piece comes home cleanly, deposits settle and payouts release smoothly.
For Borrowers

Access Wardrobes You've Been Admiring

The pieces you've been eyeing — in your circle, in your city — without adding another barely-worn item to your own cupboard. Borrow for a wedding. For a work trip. For a night you'll remember.

01
Browse Circles & Private Closets
Filter by occasion, size, and Mumbai neighbourhood — or explore private closet lookbooks shared by friends. Ask Tara anytime about fit, temporary alterations, or care rules.
02
Verify Once & See Full Pricing Upfront
Complete a quick one-time Aadhaar QR check. Pick your dates and see the full breakdown upfront — rental price, refundable security deposit, and transparent platform fee with no surprises.
03
Wear. Return. Auto-Refund.
Capture quick pickup and return photos inside your booking chat. Once your outfit is returned as received, undisputed deposits refund automatically and your community reputation grows.
Built on Trust

What If Something Goes Wrong?

Every exchange is designed to protect both women equally — clear identity checks, photo proof at handover, and refundable deposits so relationships stay warm and stress-free.

01

Official Aadhaar QR Check

Every transacting member verifies through official Aadhaar QR before lending, borrowing, or posting — keeping the circle real, women-only, and accountable.

02

Pickup & Return Photos

Both sides capture condition photos at pickup and return right inside your booking chat, so there is always a clear, shared record if a question ever comes up.

03

Protected Deposits & Care Support

Refundable security deposits refund automatically once your piece comes home cleanly. If there is ever damage or disagreement, ask Tara or let Maahu's team review and step in fairly.
