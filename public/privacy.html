Privacy Policy

How Maahu AI Digital Private Limited collects, uses, protects, and deletes personal data across maahu.ai and the Maahu mobile applications.

Effective Date: November 2026 · Data Fiduciary: Maahu AI Digital Private Limited (Mumbai, India)

Privacy & Grievance Contact: Email hello@maahu.ai for data access, correction, consent withdrawal, or account deletion inquiries.

1. Information We Collect

  • Waitlist & Early Access (maahu.ai): Name, email address, Mumbai neighbourhood, and participation intent submitted via the Founding Squad form.
  • Account & Profile: Phone number (authenticated via Firebase Phone Auth), display name, city/neighbourhood, delivery addresses, and optional wardrobe preferences.
  • Identity Verification (Aadhaar Secure QR): Cryptographic verification results extracted from UIDAI-signed Secure QR codes strictly to verify real identity and marketplace eligibility. Maahu does not collect selfie biometrics or store raw Aadhaar numbers unnecessarily.
  • Digital Closet & Listings: Garment photographs, care notes, pricing/purchase figures entered by you, and AI-assisted metadata tags generated via Google Cloud Vertex AI (confirmed by you).
  • Bookings, Messaging & Evidence: Booking timelines, payment transaction references (processed through Razorpay), real-time booking messages, and pickup/return condition photos used solely for transaction execution and dispute resolution.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate your Digital Closet, compute Wardrobe Financial ROI metrics, and provide Tara AI support responses.
  • To prevent fraud, enforce women-only verified community access, and protect bookings through handover evidence and automated deposit settlement.
  • To process payments, refunds, and lender payouts in compliance with Indian financial and tax regulations.
  • To send transactional notifications (booking requests, handover reminders, refund status) and launch updates when opted in.

3. Cloud Processing & Subprocessors

Maahu runs on Google Cloud infrastructure (primarily asia-south1, Mumbai) and Firebase services (Authentication, Firestore, App Check, Cloud Storage, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging). Payment processing and payouts are handled by Razorpay in accordance with RBI-compliant payment standards. We never sell your personal data.

4. Retention & Deletion Rights (DPDP Act)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, you may request access, correction, or erasure of your personal data at any time. Initiating account deletion removes active profile access immediately and deletes personal data once active bookings, open disputes, or statutory financial/tax audit retention windows close. See our Account Deletion Guide for step-by-step instructions.

5. Security Measures

Data in transit is encrypted via TLS, private evidence media uses short-lived signed URLs, and mobile API access is protected by Firebase App Check attestation and database-enforced authorization rules.
